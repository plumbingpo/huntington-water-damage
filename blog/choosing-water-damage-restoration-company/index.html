Choosing a Water Damage Restoration Company in Huntington, WV

Home / Blog / How to Find the Right Restoration Company in Huntington, WV

Picking a restoration company is a major decision for any Huntington, WV owner dealing with water damage. Your out-of-pocket cost, the pace of the work, the thoroughness of the repair, and the odds of secondary trouble such as mold months later all depend on who you hire. Unfortunately, the restoration industry has its share of unqualified operators who may leave you with incomplete work, inflated bills, or worse. This guide helps you identify the qualities that separate excellent restoration companies from the rest.

Confirm the Crew Is IICRC Certified

The Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC) is the gold standard for restoration industry credentials. To earn certification, a technician trains rigorously in restoration science, covering mold prevention, moisture measurement, structural drying, and proper extraction technique. Ask any restoration company you're considering whether their technicians hold current IICRC certifications in Water Damage Restoration (WRT) and Applied Structural Drying (ASD). If a company cannot prove those credentials, move on.

Make Sure They Are Insured and Licensed

Every restoration company working on your property should carry comprehensive insurance including general liability and workers' compensation. Have them send certificates of insurance and confirm the dates are current. A company lacking coverage can leave you on the hook when someone gets hurt or more property is damaged mid-project. Check as well that it has every business license Huntington or West Virginia rules call for.

Evaluate Response Time and Availability

A water loss gets worse the longer it waits. Each hour lost adds to both the damage and the bill. Look for a company that truly answers emergencies 24/7 and can get to your area quickly. Find out their typical response time for Huntington, and verify the emergency number goes to a real person, since an answering service or voicemail only adds delay.

Look at Their Equipment and Drying Process

Without the right machines, a restoration job cannot be done properly. Find out what they extract water with (truck-mounted units beat portable-only setups), which dehumidifiers they run (the industry standard is LGR), and if thermal imaging is used to find moisture. Daily moisture readings, with equipment adjusted to match them, are the mark of a reputable firm, so question any company that simply drops off machines and hopes.

Read Reviews and Call References

You can learn a lot about past performance from what customers post online. Check Google, Yelp, and the BBB for consistent patterns of quality, professionalism, and customer satisfaction. Focus on comments covering the finished quality of the work, insurance assistance, whether crews were on time, and how well the company communicated. It is also fair to ask for the names of recent Huntington water damage customers you can contact.

Know How They Price and Bill Insurance

Expect a detailed estimate up front, along with a plain explanation of the pricing, from any firm worth hiring. Good firms often price jobs with industry-standard software such as Xactimate, which insurers know and accept. Find out if they deal with insurance carriers directly and can send the bill straight to your insurer. Be wary of any company that wants a large payment up front or will not work with insurance.
